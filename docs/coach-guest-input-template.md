## Fixed Global Rules

- Keep YW NutriTech branding consistent.
- Keep global medical disclaimer.
- Keep terms, privacy, refund/payment policy, support, and footer consistent.
- Use safe health language: support, improve, work towards, lifestyle guidance, under guidance, may help.
- Avoid unsafe claims: guaranteed cure, stop medicines, no doctor needed, permanent result for everyone.
- Before marking any page ready, run build, route, funnel isolation, and multi-device visual checks.
- Visual checks must cover mobile, tablet, desktop, and large screen.
- Confirm readable text, tappable CTAs/buttons, unstretched images/logos, centered premium loader, smooth animations, proper footer/legal display, no horizontal scrolling, and correct coach-specific funnel isolation on every device.
- Confirm the hero section is visible immediately on page open across mobile, tablet, desktop, and large screen.
- Confirm the main hook, coach identity, and primary CTA are not pushed too far down by spacing, banners, loader behavior, or oversized visuals.
