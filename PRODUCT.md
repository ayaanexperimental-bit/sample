# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is the YW Coach root owner: an accountable operator who moves between analytics, coach-site lifecycle, Shop and payment operations, reporting, settings, incidents, and Admin-user governance. The owner needs a complete operational picture, explicit risk boundaries, and verifiable outcomes during long, high-consequence sessions.

Secondary users are permission-limited admins working in one or more assigned areas such as analytics, Website Creator, Shop, or support. They must see only the modules, records, evidence, and actions allowed by current RBAC. Desktop is the primary dense-work environment; mobile and tablet remain fully usable for review, navigation, approvals, and bounded actions.

## Product Purpose

YW Coach Admin is the authenticated operating surface for managing coach sites, business analytics, reports, payments, Shop workflows, support and incident queues, settings, and Admin access. Its contextual Copilot helps operators understand evidence, investigate anomalies, assemble reports, and prepare safe actions without becoming the authority for permissions, payment truth, validation, or destructive execution.

Success means an authorized operator can identify the current state, understand what requires attention, complete or safely hand off the intended task, and verify the result through source notes, receipts, and audit history without leaving the Admin workspace. This context governs the `/admin` product surface; public coach funnels retain their own design requirements and visual systems.

## Positioning

One evidence-first, permission-aware control room where AI accelerates operational decisions while deterministic product controls remain authoritative.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, accountable. The interface is alert without being theatrical, technically credible without becoming cryptic, and direct without sounding abrupt. It earns trust through honest states, visible evidence, and predictable controls rather than decorative intensity.

## Anti-references

- A generic AI chatbot or autonomous-agent spectacle that competes with the operator's task.
- A marketing-style SaaS dashboard built from hero metrics, decorative gradients, glass panels, or repeated promotional cards.
- Mock, prototype, placeholder, or fabricated production data presented as real.
- Legacy Admin wrappers, hidden old mounts, stale labels, dead controls, or visual skins that conceal an older interface.
- Dense walls of simultaneously expanded commands, context, guidance, and technical detail.
- Model output acting as permission, payment, validation, calculation, OTP, route-protection, or destructive-action authority.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence before action.** Show source, freshness, uncertainty, and the expected effect before asking for a decision.
2. **Progressively disclose complexity.** Keep the next operator decision visible; preserve commands, context, safeguards, and observability on demand.
3. **Make permission boundaries legible.** Navigation, data, actions, and explanations must reflect current RBAC without leaking hidden capability names or records.
4. **Use honest operational states.** Loading, empty, blocked, stale, partial, unavailable, success, and failure states must describe reality and never imply unsupported completion.
5. **Keep AI subordinate to the workflow.** Copilot may explain, plan, retrieve, and recommend; deterministic services authorize, execute, confirm, and audit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG conformance level is asserted by the current product contract. The required operational floor is keyboard access, visible focus, semantic navigation and dialogs, accessible names and status announcements, focus restoration, reduced-motion behavior, readable light and dark themes, non-color-only status cues, and touch targets of at least 44px where the responsive interface requires them. The Admin surface must remain usable without horizontal overflow from 320px through large desktop viewports.
